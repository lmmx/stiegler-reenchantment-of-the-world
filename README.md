*Bernard Stiegler & Ars Industrialis, **Réenchanter le monde. La valeur esprit contre le populisme industriel** (Flammarion, 2006); **The Re-Enchantment of the World: The Value of Spirit against Industrial Populism**, trans. Trevor Arthur (Bloomsbury Academic, 2014).*

## The thesis in one sentence

Consumer capitalism has captured desire and attention through the industries of communication and culture ("industrial populism"). In doing so it lowers "the value of spirit" and turns the promised knowledge society into a reign of ignorance. To re-enchant the world, the same technologies must become "technologies of spirit" that support knowledge, care and individuation, under an industrial politics led by a renewed public power.

## The book in one paragraph

In 2005 the French employers' federation, Medef, held its summer university under the slogan "the re-enchantment of the world" to promote the knowledge economy. Stiegler and the newly founded association **Ars Industrialis** "take it at its word". Re-enchantment requires reversing the "drop in the value of spirit" Paul Valéry noted in 1939. That drop is now organized by a **libidinal economy** in which television and marketing sell "available brain time". The result exhausts desire into drives and **proletarianizes** producers, consumers and knowledge workers alike, stripping them of *savoir-faire*, *savoir-vivre* and theoretical knowledge. Because human memory and knowledge have always been technical (**grammatization**, **tertiary retention**), the answer is not to reject technology. It is to invest, publicly and over the long term, in instruments, institutions and practices that let people re-appropriate their technologies of memory. The book gathers the association's 2005 manifesto (and, in English, its 2010 manifesto, which reframes the programme as a **pharmacology** of care and an **economy of contribution**), two essays by Stiegler on the "re-foundation of society" and "against the reign of ignorance", and a motion addressed to the 2005 UN summit on the information society in Tunis.
