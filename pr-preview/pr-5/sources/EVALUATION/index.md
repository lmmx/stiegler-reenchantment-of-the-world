# Evaluation of sources

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/index.md>) › **Source evaluation** · see also [reception.md](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/reception/index.md>)

Each source in `sources/` is rated on five criteria:

- **Accuracy:** against the book itself (see [STRUCTURE.md](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/STRUCTURE/index.md>)).
- **Coverage:** which parts of the book it treats: P = Preface, I = Introduction, M05 / M10 = Manifestos, C1 / C2 = Chapters, T = Tunis motion.
- **Depth:** how far the source goes, from 1 (notice) to 5 (sustained analysis).
- **Stance:** interpretive bias.
- **Reliability:** high / medium / low.

The files themselves hold the condensed account and fuller notes.

## E. Cross-source disagreements

| Issue | Positions | Our assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the remedy empty? | Lotz: "empty placeholders". Lyons: "philosophy of hope" but no concrete answers. Lemmens interview: a "new industrial model" with policy planks | Both reviewers under-weight the 2010 Manifesto §9 policies and Ch. 2's "practical consequences". Those are concrete but sectoral (education, media, R&amp;D instruments) rather than an account of political agency |
| Marx | Lotz, Hutnyk, Manche: class and accumulation sidelined. Ross, Lemmens: return to the *Grundrisse* and the knowledge sense of proletarianization | A real disagreement of frameworks; see [concepts/proletarianization.md](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/concepts/proletarianization/index.md>) |
| Spirit | Roodt: reduced to libido. De Beer: spiritual depth. Abbinnett v. Beardsworth: memorial v. futural. Rossouw: theological | The book's own definition is noetic and transindividual and explicitly not only religious (Tunis motion) |
| Digital networks | 2010 Manifesto: associated milieu. Stiegler 2018: "systemically dissociating" | The author revised his view; read the 2010 text historically |
| Authorship / founders | EN Wikipedia: three co-authors. Catalogues and association: five founders | Five (Collins, Crépon, Perret, B. Stiegler, C. Stiegler) |
| Tunis motion | Lyons, blurbs: "at the summit", "account of the summit". Association: press motion on the eve | The association's account is correct |
