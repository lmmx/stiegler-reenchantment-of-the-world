# Stiegler, "Economizing means taking care" (2008)

A web essay. No translator is credited on the page, and the text has no pagination. Hutnyk (2012) cites it as the epigraph source for "psycho-power" (see lit-hutnyk-proletarianisation-2012.md).

## Condensed account

- **Care / economy.** "Economy" is taken in its etymological sense of the household: to economise is to take care. Attention is both psychic and collective, and paying attention means taking care. An industrial system could be a system of care, with care as its value chain, instead of consuming its own resources.
- **Psychopower.** It is the systematic capture of attention by psychotechnologies (radio, TV, digital). Marketing is its organ, it succeeds Foucault's biopower, and it destroys the identification between parents and children. The counter-concept is a "noopolitics" built on "technologies of spirit" that support sublimation.
- **Libidinal economy.** Consumerism channels libido into drives that destroy their objects, whereas desire cares for and sustains its object. The capitalist libidinal economy therefore "self-destructs".
- **Three limits of capitalism.** These are the falling rate of profit, the destruction of libidinal energy, and environmental toxicity. Rifkin's hydrogen economy is insufficient if it ignores libidinal energy. The same three-point scheme appears in the Ars Industrialis "Mécroissance" entry.
- **Proletarianization.** It affects producers and consumers alike. The production/consumption opposition is its structural cause, and overcoming it is the key to any exit from crisis. (Contribution is implied but not elaborated on this page.)
- **Short-termism.** Speculative finance indifferent to its objects, and a short-term way of life, destroy the capacity for long-term projection, which the ecological crisis requires.
- **Pharmacology.** Tertiary retentions can heal or harm. Every attentional technology can be reversed to dissociate attention, and social networks can build or short-circuit collective individuation.
- **Otium / skholè.** "Ascendant" innovation coming from users is linked to skholè and otium (leisure devoted to knowledge and the art of living), set against the production/consumption model.

## Short quotations (Stiegler)

- "to economise is to take care"
- "Psycho-power is the systematic organisation of the capture of attention"
- "the capitalist libidinal economy self-destructs"
- "The loss of attention is a loss of capacities of projection into the long term"

## Notes for evaluation

- **Relation to the book:** this is the closest freely available English text to *Réenchanter*'s programme. It shares the call for a European industrial politics of "technologies of spirit", the diagnosis that the consumerist libidinal economy is self-destroying, and the link between the investment of capital and libido. It is later (post-2008) and adds the pharmacological and psychopower vocabulary, along with the 2008 crisis.
- **Reliability:** an authorial text, so authoritative for Stiegler's views. The English is unattributed and unpaginated. Cite with the date caveat.
