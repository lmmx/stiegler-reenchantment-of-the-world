# Hypomnemata (*hypomnèse*, *hypomnémata*)

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-5/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **hypomnemata**

## Definition

Greek: **memory aids**, supports of memory outside the mind. Examples are notebooks, account books, registers and writing in general. In the *Phaedrus*, Plato contrasts ***hypomnesis*** (artificial recall) with ***anamnesis*** (living recollection, the true knowledge of the soul). **Foucault** ("L'écriture de soi", 1983) showed how Stoic and Epicurean *hypomnemata* served as techniques of the self, a "writing of the self". Stiegler joins the two senses. All mnemotechnics, from the alphabet to digital media, are *hypomnemata*. They can support anamnesis and self-constitution or replace them, which makes them *pharmaka*.

## Disagreements

- **Labelled interpretation:** the book's core move against Plato is that hypomnesis is the *condition* of anamnesis, not its contrary, which is a Derridean inheritance. Ch. 2 states it in policy terms: build institutions of appropriation.
