# Ars Industrialis: what the association's own site says

## Condensed account

- **Name.** "Association internationale pour une politique industrielle des technologies de l'esprit".
- **Self-description ("qui sommes-nous ?").** The page opens with a quotation from Demy's *Les Demoiselles de Rochefort* ("Nous sommes des sœurs jumelles…"). The founders describe themselves as "au départ quatre philosophes et une juriste", "cinq" at the initiative of the association. They are worried by the "désarroi" in industrial countries, especially France. Their working hypothesis is that a decisive element of the contemporary *malaise* (in Freud's sense) is a mutation in the **technologies of spirit**, the "hypomnémata" of our time (after Foucault's "L'écriture de soi"). They want to be a "force de proposition, et non seulement de réflexion". Thought and the life of the spirit consist first in **combating stupidity (bêtise)**, beginning with one's own, and the "forces of regression" that encourage it.
- **The founders** (from the inaugural minutes and the Tunis motion): **George Collins, Marc Crépon, Catherine Perret, Bernard Stiegler** (philosophers) and **Caroline Stiegler** (jurist).
- **Public sessions** were held at the Théâtre de la Colline (Paris), usually on Saturday afternoons, roughly quarterly. Up to the book's publication (Oct 2006) and just after: 18 June 2005 (inaugural); 24 Sept 2005 (knowledge vs information society: the Tunis summit); 5 Nov 2005 (cognitive technologies and digital libraries); 25 Feb 2006 (suffering and consumption); 20 May 2006 (politics and television); 14 Oct 2006 (public impotence and political power); 16 Dec 2006 (sustainable investment); 20 Jan 2007 (participatory democracy); May–June 2007 (formation and destruction of attention). A parallel seminar, "Trouver de nouvelles armes. Pour une polémologie de l'esprit", ran at the Collège international de philosophie for about four years.
- **2020\.** A convocation signed by Bernard Stiegler calls an extraordinary general assembly on **27 February 2020** to amend the statutes and found the "**Association des Amis de la Génération Thunberg**". It refers to the Internation group's work, presented in Geneva on 10 January 2020 to Antonio Guterres and Greta Thunberg. The site footer reads "arsindustrialis.org – 2008-2012". The site is now essentially an archive and links to "Épokhè".

## Short quotations

- "au départ quatre philosophes et une juriste".
- Thought "consiste d'abord à combattre. Combattre la bêtise, et d'abord sa propre bêtise".

## Notes for evaluation

- This is the authoritative source for the founders and the session chronology, and it settles the co-founder question (five people, including Caroline Stiegler). Compare [media-wikipedia-stiegler-ars-industrialis-2026.md](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/media-wikipedia-stiegler-ars-industrialis-2026/index.md>).
- everybodywiki ("Ars industrialis") claims the association was **renamed in 2017**. The association's own 2020 convocation points to a **2020** statutory change instead.
- The site is partisan by nature. It records what the association did, not how others received it.
