# Son\[i\]a \#285, Bernard Stiegler (MACBA, 2019)

A podcast interview in which Stiegler speaks at length on the programme that this book founded. The episode page gives a description and a timeline of topics.

## Condensed account

- Stiegler is introduced as head of IRI (Centre Pompidou), founder of Ars Industrialis, and theorist of the "proletarianisation of knowledge".
- His framing: technology as a starting point for thinking about living together. Technics can raise or lower **entropy**, which makes it poison or cure (**pharmacology**).
- His proposal: a new model of knowledge and a large-scale **contributory economy** building collective intelligence against the Anthropocene, robotics and the quantification of life.
- Timeline headings include "Contributory economy: work vs proletarianization", "Refounding Knowledge", "Digital pharmakon", "The need of an economic peace", "Macroeconomics and Neganthropic bifurcation" and "Degrowth is the wrong question".

## Notes for evaluation

- **Relation to the book:** late (2019) and in the vocabulary of entropy and neganthropology. Still, "Refounding knowledge" and "degrowth is the wrong question" restate positions already in the 2006 book: Ch. 2's knowledge-against-information argument, and the Introduction's call for "new, qualitative growth" rather than mere reduction.
- **Reliability:** primary voice. The description and timeline give themes, not specific arguments.
