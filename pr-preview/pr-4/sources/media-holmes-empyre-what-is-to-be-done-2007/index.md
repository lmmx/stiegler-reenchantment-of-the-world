# Brian Holmes on -empyre- (January 2007)

## Condensed account

In a new-media art mailing-list thread titled "what is to be done?", Holmes replies to a post asking what to do about a process that shaped our sense of the metaphysical but is now run by machines. He says such a post needs proposals, and that Western philosophy offers little to respond with. He then turns to **Stiegler, whom he is reading at that moment**, and gives a compact summary. Advanced capitalism is above all cultural, built on devices that externalise memory and creativity. Such devices are indispensable, but their industrial production must be shaped so that people can keep "individuating" in tension with society. Holmes connects Stiegler's "writing machines" to Foucault's "writing of the self". Europe should redirect these technologies to protect singularity. Without such a change, Stiegler expects disenchantment with democracy to spread, possibly to violent outbursts. Holmes notes that Stiegler's latest book, *Réenchanter le monde: La valeur esprit contre le populisme industriel*, opens with a chapter called "What is to be done?". He asks the original poster how they would answer it.

## Short quotations

- "There is, effectively, nothing in the Western philosophical tradition that will help respond."
- "So I am quite curious what you think is to be done."

## Notes for evaluation

- This is **very early English-language reception**, three months after the French publication and seven years before the translation, by a well-known practitioner-critic in the tactical-media and net-art scene. It shows the book circulating among art and media activists.
- The summary is accurate in outline. The book's intro is indeed "Que faire ?" / "What Is to Be Done?", and the Europe and individuation framing matches. The prediction of violent outbursts reflects Stiegler's warnings in this period (e.g. after the 2005 banlieue riots), but Holmes does not cite a page. The Foucault "writing of the self" link is genuinely Ars Industrialis's: the "qui sommes-nous" page cites "L'écriture de soi".
- It is an informal list post and not a review.
