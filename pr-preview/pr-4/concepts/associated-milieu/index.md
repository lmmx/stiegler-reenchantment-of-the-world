# Associated milieu / dissociated milieu (*milieu associé / dissocié*)

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **associated milieu**

## Definition

**Gilbert Simondon** (*Du mode d'existence des objets techniques*, 1958) called a technical object's **associated milieu** the environment that the object itself conditions and that conditions it in return, in a recursive loop. His example is the Guimbal turbine, cooled by the very river that drives it. Stiegler transposes the idea to **social and symbolic milieus**. A milieu is **associated** when its participants contribute to individuating it and individuate themselves in doing so: language, a scientific community, free-software projects. It is **dissociated** (Stiegler's coinage) when it is individuated by specialists at the expense of everyone else. The culture industries are the paradigm: transmitters and receivers, producers and consumers.

## Where it appears

- [2010 Manifesto](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/03-manifesto-2010/index.md>) §3: on the internet, contributors "form what one calls an **associated milieu**—thereby taking up a concept from Gilbert Simondon". §7: digital infrastructure as an "industrial and technogeographical associated milieu". Its note 9 refers back to *Réenchanter le monde* (2006) for the concept.
- [Ch. 1](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/04-ch1-re-foundation-of-society/index.md>): **central**. Language as the model associated milieu (pp. 34, 49); Simondon's concept and the Guimbal turbine (p. 36); dissociated milieus are "ANTISOCIAL" (p. 36); the internet "structurally constitutes an associated milieu", unlike earlier industrial apparatuses (p. 37); the American revival of associated milieus (p. 45); the *polis* reconstitutes an "as-sociated milieu" through literal writing (p. 49); public power and the European way of life (pp. 53–55).
- [Ch. 2](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance/index.md>): **Minitel** "inaugurated the age of associated milieus founded upon an industrial technology" (p. 61). Tele-cracy is "the perfect accomplishment of the dissociated milieu" (p. 62). Only associated milieus let "the life of the spirit" blossom (p. 67). The knowledge society is "the generalization of associated milieus" (p. 69). Associated milieus are "the a priori condition of any society worthy of the name" (p. 75). Push vs pull in TV/IP convergence (pp. 81–82). The library is an associated milieu par excellence (p. 86). "Societies of authors and readers" against the split of transmitters and receivers (p. 91). N. 3: a human milieu is only ever *relatively* associated.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Stiegler himself** (*The Neganthropocene*, notes 271 and 449) cites *The Re-Enchantment of the World* (2014) as the place where he analyses "the recent evolution" of Simondon's associated technogeographic milieus, "which Simondon never had the opportunity to analyse". This is **authorial confirmation** that the concept is central to the book ([Ross file](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018/index.md>)).
- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire*:** "dissociated milieu" is credited to Stiegler. The internet is "not structurally dissociated", which makes the contributory economy possible ([glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013/index.md>)).
- **Lemmens (2017):** clear on the concept; dissociation is still "the dominant situation today" ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-lemmens-digital-pharmakon-general-intellect-2017/index.md>)).
- **Hui &amp; Halpin (2013):** a design programme for social networks as collective individuation ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-hui-halpin-collective-individuation-2013/index.md>)).

## Disagreements

- **Stiegler against his 2006–2010 self:** later (2018, p. 100) he argues the reticulated web has become "a systemically dissociating milieu, because it is hyper-proletarianizing". The book's hope is thus revised by its author.
- **Labelled interpretation:** whether a platform is "associated" turns on who sets the rules (reciprocity, the reader able to write). That is why the 2010 Manifesto ties the concept to free software rather than to "Web 2.0" in general.
