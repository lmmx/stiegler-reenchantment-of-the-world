# Technologies of spirit (*technologies de l'esprit*); psychotechnologies vs nootechnologies

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **technologies of spirit**

## Definition (the book's usage)

The **2005 Manifesto** coined "technologies of spirit" for the sector formed when digitization makes the **culture industries, program industries, media, telecommunications and cognitive technologies converge**. These technologies are *spiritual* because they work on attention, memory, imagination and desire. They are also ***hypomnemata***. Ars Industrialis is defined as an association "for an industrial politics of technologies of spirit". The 2010 Manifesto sharpens the point into a normative pair. **Psychotechnologies** serve behavioural control (marketing, audience capture). **Nootechnologies** serve the life of the mind and de-proletarianization. The same device can be either.

Nora and Minc foresaw the convergence of audiovisual, telecom and computing but not an "industrial politics of technologies of spirit" (pp. 50–51).

Pp. 84–86: Mallarmé's "The Book, a spiritual instrument"; Husserl's "spirit-invested objects"; digital hypomnemata as "new spiritual instruments".
- [Tunis motion](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/06-tunis-motion/index.md>) p. 97: "an industrial politics of technologies of spirit" as the basis for another summit.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire* ("Psychotechnique / nootechnique"):** the opposition is "normative, assumed as such". Psychotechnics act on or against the mind to control it, and nootechnics foster its individuation. The criterion is **reciprocity** (the reader can also write) and long circuits of transindividuation ([glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013/index.md>)).
- **Stiegler, "Within the limits of capitalism…" (c. 2008):** calls for a European industrial politics of technologies of spirit ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/primary-parallel-stiegler-economizing-taking-care-2008/index.md>)).
- **Stiegler, 5 Nov 2005 session** on cognitive technologies, knowledge management and design methodology ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/media-stiegler-technologies-cognitives-2005/index.md>)).
