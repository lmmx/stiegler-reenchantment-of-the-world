# Proletarianization (*prolétarisation*)

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **proletarianization**

## Definition (the book's usage)

Proletarianization is the **loss of knowledge** (*savoir*) by individuals when that knowledge is formalized in, and passes into, a technique, machine, apparatus or service. The 2010 Manifesto §6 gives the canonical definition: knowledge "being formalized through a technique, a machine, or an apparatus, can escape the individual". Three waves:

1. **Workers** lose *savoir-faire* (know-how) to machines: Smith and Marx.
2. **Consumers** lose *savoir-vivre* (knowing how to live) to apparatus and services. The TV set "occupying" children is the example, and Ch. 1 §3 has the line "mode de vie" becoming "mode d'emploi" (a way of life becoming a user manual).
3. **Cognitive workers and decision-makers** lose theoretical and critical knowledge to information systems. Greenspan admitted he did not understand the system he managed.

Ch. 1 §3 gives the short form: the proletarian is one who **loses their knowledge**. The producer becomes pure labour power, the consumer pure purchasing power.

- [Ch. 1](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/04-ch1-re-foundation-of-society/index.md>): "The consumer discharged of his existence" (p. 30). §3 (pp. 31–35): generalized proletarianization through service capitalism's control of adoption. "One who has lost his ways of knowing has been proletarianized". The producer loses *savoir-faire* and becomes labour power (*force de travail*); the consumer loses *savoir-vivre*, which becomes a mode of employment, and becomes purchasing power (*pouvoir d'achat*). Fordism generalizes this, masked by making the producer a consumer. P. 51: machines (Taylor), then appliances, exteriorize knowledge into algorithms, giving proletarianization and loss of *savoir-faire*, *savoir-vivre* and contemplation.
- [Ch. 2](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance/index.md>) pp. 71–72: losses of *savoir-faire* and *savoir-vivre* over a few decades, as losses of individuation. P. 74: digital instrumentalization proletarianizes researchers and dissociates cognitive milieus.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Ross (2018):** "perhaps his fundamental political concept". Socrates is its first thinker; the waves run nineteenth century (work), twentieth (life), twenty-first (theory) ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018/index.md>)).
- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire*:** Marx's working class was only the *first* proletarianized class. After 2008 designers and decision-makers are proletarianized by automated systems. The proletarian is "the employee of a dissociated milieu" (Simondon). Proletarianization is "not a fatality" ([glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013/index.md>)).
- **Stiegler, April interview (2011):** plain-language statement of the consumer's loss of *savoir-vivre* ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/media-april-stiegler-interview-2011/index.md>)).
- **Lotz (2015):** mental life becomes "proletarian" through externalized memory; he notes Stiegler "no longer" speaks Marxist language ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/review-lotz-marx-and-philosophy-2015/index.md>)).

## Disagreements

- **Marxist critics** (Hutnyk 2012; Manche 2021; Lotz): redefining the proletariat by **loss of knowledge** rather than **dispossession and wage-labour** sidelines class, exploitation and accumulation. Manche's title puts it sharply: "The problem is proletarianisation, not capitalism" ([Hutnyk](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-hutnyk-proletarianisation-2012/index.md>), [Manche](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-manche-proletarianisation-not-capitalism-2021/index.md>)).
- **Sympathetic readers** (Ross, Lemmens) reply that Stiegler is returning to an earlier sense found in Marx's *Grundrisse* (knowledge absorbed into fixed capital) and in Simondon ([Lemmens](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/sources/lit-lemmens-digital-pharmakon-general-intellect-2017/index.md>)).
- **Labelled interpretation:** the concept explains the book's otherwise surprising politics. If the problem is loss of knowledge rather than ownership, the remedy is de-proletarianization through new practices and instruments, which leaves the question of property in second place.
