# Individuation, transindividuation, disindividuation

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/pr-preview/pr-4/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **transindividuation**

## Definition

From **Simondon** (*L'individuation psychique et collective*). **Individuation** is the never-finished process by which an individual becomes who it is. It is always at once **psychic** (an "I") and **collective** (a "we"). Stiegler adds a third, **technical** strand, because individuation passes through artefacts and memory supports. **Transindividuation** is the co-individuation of "I"s that produces a "we": shared meanings, knowledges and institutions, metastabilized in symbols and techniques. **Disindividuation** is the breakdown of this process. It happens when psychotechnologies **short-circuit** transindividuation: they impose ready-made identifications, saturate attention, or split producers of meaning from consumers.

## Disagreements

- **Labelled interpretation:** Stiegler's adaptation thesis (individuation = **adoption**, from *Technics and Time 3*) is a distinctive addition to Simondon. It turns the theory into a theory of culture and marketing: whoever shapes adoption shapes individuation.
