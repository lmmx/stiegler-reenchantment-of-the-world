# Short-termism (*court-termisme*)

[README](../README.md) › [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) › **short-termism**

## Definition (the book's usage)

Short-termism is the systematic sacrifice of the long term to the short term in investment, management, media and politics. For Stiegler it is not only a financial vice. It is the **temporal form of drive-based behaviour**: the drive wants immediate satisfaction, whereas desire and knowledge are long-circuited and accumulate over time. Shareholder demands for "two-digit returns" (2005 Manifesto), adaptive corporate strategy, audience-ratings TV and speculative finance express one and the same short-termism. Its opposite is **investment**, financial and libidinal (*Besetzung*), and long-term public anticipation.

## Where it appears

- [2005 Manifesto](../chapters/02-manifesto-2005.md): market criteria favour "short-term financial investments"; shareholders want returns "never below 15%".
- [Ch. 2](../chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance.md) p. 66: Medef's "continuous adaptation" is an entropic vicious circle. Industrial policy for technologies of spirit can only be **long-term**, led by a new public power.
- [Tunis motion](../chapters/06-tunis-motion.md): public and private funds should not seek "short-term returns on investment".
- [2010 Manifesto](../chapters/03-manifesto-2010.md) §1: "neo-liberal short-termism" leads to disinvestment. The speculator is "a capitalist who no longer invests". §5: guarantee the short term in order to reach and **reverse** it in the long term.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire* ("Financiarisation"):** finance as a "commerce of promises". Fully realized short-termism destroys the retentions (knowledge) and protentions (investments) of time (quoting *For a New Critique of Political Economy*, 2009) ([glossary](../sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013.md)).
- **Stiegler, "Pharmacology of Desire" (2011):** drive-based consumption mirrors speculative disinvestment, with hedge funds and LBOs as examples ([source](../sources/primary-parallel-stiegler-pharmacology-of-desire-2011.md)).
- **Hansen (2014):** the "short-term economy of the drive" ([source](../sources/lit-hansen-saving-libidinal-capitalism-2014.md)).
- **Ross (2018):** the financial crisis as a symptom of increasingly short-term, speculative investment ([source](../sources/lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018.md)).

## Disagreements

- Little disagreement on the diagnosis. The **critics' question** is about agency: who will impose the long term? Stiegler's answer, a "new public power" with industry, is what Lotz and Manche find reformist and Lemmens finds statist ([reception.md](../reception.md)).
