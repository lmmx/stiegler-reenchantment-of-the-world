# Individuation, transindividuation, disindividuation

[Home](../README.md) › [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) › **transindividuation**

## Definition

From **Simondon** (*L'individuation psychique et collective*). **Individuation** is the never-finished process by which an individual becomes who it is. It is always at once **psychic** (an "I") and **collective** (a "we"). Stiegler adds a third, **technical** strand, because individuation passes through artefacts and memory supports. **Transindividuation** is the co-individuation of "I"s that produces a "we": shared meanings, knowledges and institutions, metastabilized in symbols and techniques. **Disindividuation** is the breakdown of this process. It happens when psychotechnologies **short-circuit** transindividuation: they impose ready-made identifications, saturate attention, or split producers of meaning from consumers.

## Where it appears

- [Translator's Preface](../chapters/00-translators-preface.md): individuation ("one becomes who he is") and transindividuation ("we become who we are").
- [Ch. 1](../chapters/04-ch1-re-foundation-of-society.md) p. 30: service industries short-circuit transindividuation; n. 7: individuation is not individualization. §3 (pp. 31–33): **individuation is adoption**, and service capitalism destroys individuation by controlling adoption (primary and secondary identification). P. 34: Simondon (*IPC* p. 18) on the individual as an element in a vaster individuation, with **language** as the model of participatory psychic and collective individuation. P. 47: "Grammatization and individuation". P. 57 (quoted by Lotz): hyper-solicitation of attention "short-circuits transindividuation", causing dis-identification and **disindividuation**. N. 49: the double sense of "disaffected".
- [Ch. 2](../chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance.md) p. 71: spirit is a matter of motives, so of psychic and collective individuation. P. 81: "**The risk of disindividuation** as the growth of ignorance rather than knowledge". Individuation is always psychic *and* collective; knowledge exists only as shared and public.

## Disagreements

- **Labelled interpretation:** Stiegler's adaptation thesis (individuation = **adoption**, from *Technics and Time 3*) is a distinctive addition to Simondon. It turns the theory into a theory of culture and marketing: whoever shapes adoption shapes individuation.
