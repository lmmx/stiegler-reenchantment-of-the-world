# Care (*soin*; *prendre soin*; *epimeleia*)

[README](../README.md) › [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) › **care**

## Definition (the book's usage)

Care is attention to and concern for oneself, others and the world, sustained by disciplines and **techniques of the self** (Greek *melete* and *epimeleia*, after Foucault). It is the **therapeutic** that keeps a *pharmakon* from becoming toxic. Its opposite is ***incurie***, "carelessness" or negligence. The 2010 Manifesto makes care the axiom of political economy: "to economize means first of all and before anything else to take care" (§2). A politics, and so a political economy, is "firstly and above all a system of care" (§4.2). Culture is what "cultivates a caring relation to the pharmaka".

## Where it appears

- [2010 Manifesto](../chapters/03-manifesto-2010.md) §2 (carelessness of the addicted; care to the heart of political economy), §4.2 (system of care), §5 (a new industrial economy "founded on care"), §7 (care as the "ethics" of contributory business).
- [Translator's Preface](../chapters/00-translators-preface.md): *savoir-vivre* as "care for oneself and for sociality".
- In the 2006 chapters, care is present mainly as **responsibility**, **attention** and the **school** as an institution of appropriation ([Ch. 2](../chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance.md) pp. 79–91). The systematic theme dates from *Prendre soin* (2008), so it is anachronistic for the 2006 texts.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Stiegler, "Within the limits of capitalism, economizing means taking care" (c. 2008):** the source text of the 2010 axiom ([source](../sources/primary-parallel-stiegler-economizing-taking-care-2008.md)).
- **Cohen (2017):** the best secondary account. She links care to Foucault's *epimeleia heautou* and calls Stiegler's later project a "mystagogy" ([source](../sources/lit-cohen-dare-to-care-2017.md)).
- **Ross (2018):** culture, education and knowledge are "practices of care" that metastabilize transindividuation ([source](../sources/lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018.md)).
- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire*:** the pharmakon is both what enables care and what must be cared for ([glossary](../sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013.md)).

## Disagreements

- **Lotz (2015):** the emphasis on "responsibility, care, spirit, and education" shows a position "idealistic and ethical" at its core, a reformist social-democratic ethics in place of political economy ([source](../sources/review-lotz-marx-and-philosophy-2015.md)).
- **Cohen (2017)** is sympathetic but corrective. She uses Foucault's aesthetics of existence to temper Stiegler's more prescriptive mode.
- **Labelled interpretation:** the play on "economy" in French (*économiser*, to save or spare, to look after) carries much of the 2010 axiom's force. In English the link between economizing and caring is less evident.
