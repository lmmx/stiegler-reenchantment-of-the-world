# Otium / negotium

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **otium / negotium**

## Definition

Latin terms. ***Negotium*** ("not-leisure") is business: the sphere of subsistence, calculation and exchange. ***Otium*** is studious leisure: time freed from necessity for cultivating oneself and others, reading, writing, contemplation and care of the self. In Greek this is *skholè*, from which "school" derives. Stiegler holds that the two must stay **distinct without being opposed**. Both use *hypomnemata*. Otium aims at "consistences" (ideal objects, the objects of sublimation), and its practices are disciplines or techniques of the self. His charge against service capitalism is that it **folds otium into negotium**: leisure becomes consumption time, and "free" time is sold as available brain time.

## Where it appears

- [2005 Manifesto](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/02-manifesto-2005/index.md>) p. 13: industrial technologies of spirit, as new hypomnemata like those of the Stoics and Epicureans or of "the Rome in which the Greek *skholè* became the Roman practice of the *otium*", call for new practices. Note 5 cites J.-M. André, *L'Otium dans la vie morale et intellectuelle romaine* (1966). The manifesto also mentions Foucault's "writing of the self" (*hypomnemata* in Stoic and Epicurean schools), which is the practice of otium.
- [Ch. 1](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/04-ch1-re-foundation-of-society/index.md>) develops the related scheme of **subsistence, existence and consistence**. Man cannot merely subsist; he ex-sists (p. 30). Modes of subsistence and existence must support "the other plane", the plane of consistencies, which "do not exist" and must be cared for (pp. 58–60).
- Stiegler's *Libération* diary (June 2005), written in the founding weeks of Ars Industrialis, makes otium explicit. Freedom passes "essentially through *otium*", and otium is equated with **spirit** (sacred or profane), linked to Plutarch's "art of living" and to the journal as a practice of self-writing ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/primary-parallel-stiegler-liberation-diary-2005/index.md>)).

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire*:** negotium is the calculable sphere of business and subsistence; otium is leisure and existence, oriented to consistences and practised through techniques of the self. Service industries short-circuit otium. See also the entries "Skholè" and "Subsister, exister, consister" ([glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013/index.md>)).
- **Cohen (2017):** the subsistence/existence/consistence scheme and care of the self (Foucault) ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/lit-cohen-dare-to-care-2017/index.md>)).
- **Abbinnett (2015):** in the contributory economy, freed from "ratio", otium becomes "the global-universal good of prosthetic humanity" ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/lit-abbinnett-politics-of-spirit-2015/index.md>)).
- **Rossouw (2015):** the "new otium" as a secularized Christian project ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/lit-rossouw-aestheology-new-otium-2015/index.md>)).

## Disagreements

- Abbinnett glosses otium as "**the labour of spirit**". **Labelled interpretation:** this is loose, since otium is precisely *not* labour in the sense of negotium. A charitable reading would say Stiegler distinguishes *work* (*travail*, *œuvre*) from *employment*, so that otium can be "work" without being employment, as in the 2010 Manifesto §9.3.
- Rossouw's theological reading against the secular reading of Ars Industrialis remains open.
