# Care (*soin*; *prendre soin*; *epimeleia*)

[Home](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/index.md>) › [Glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/GLOSSARY/index.md>) › **care**

## Definition (the book's usage)

Care is attention to and concern for oneself, others and the world, sustained by disciplines and **techniques of the self** (Greek *melete* and *epimeleia*, after Foucault). It is the **therapeutic** that keeps a *pharmakon* from becoming toxic. Its opposite is ***incurie***, "carelessness" or negligence. The 2010 Manifesto makes care the axiom of political economy: "to economize means first of all and before anything else to take care" (§2). A politics, and so a political economy, is "firstly and above all a system of care" (§4.2). Culture is what "cultivates a caring relation to the pharmaka".

## Where it appears

- [Translator's Preface](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/00-translators-preface/index.md>): *savoir-vivre* as "care for oneself and for sociality".
- [2010 Manifesto](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/03-manifesto-2010/index.md>) §2 (carelessness of the addicted; care to the heart of political economy), §4.2 (system of care), §5 (a new industrial economy "founded on care"), §7 (care as the "ethics" of contributory business).
- [Ch. 1](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/04-ch1-re-foundation-of-society/index.md>): traditional societies "take care" of libidinal energy (*philia*, *eros*, *agape*, *caritas*, *mana*) (p. 43). Care is the "political question par excellence", understood as **socio-therapy**, the care taken of individuation (p. 47). The stakes are "(a) founding then (b) caring for" individuation (p. 51). Public power is the care of the social dynamic (p. 54). Consistencies must be treated "with the greatest care" by a libidinal *eco-nomy* (p. 60).
- [Ch. 2](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/chapters/05-ch2-against-the-reign-of-ignorance/index.md>) adds responsibility, attention and the school (pp. 79–91).
- *Prendre soin* (2008) systematizes the theme; it does not introduce it.

## How secondary sources gloss it

- **Stiegler, "Within the limits of capitalism, economizing means taking care" (c. 2008):** the source text of the 2010 axiom ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/primary-parallel-stiegler-economizing-taking-care-2008/index.md>)).
- **Cohen (2017):** the best secondary account. She links care to Foucault's *epimeleia heautou* and calls Stiegler's later project a "mystagogy" ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/lit-cohen-dare-to-care-2017/index.md>)).
- **Ross (2018):** culture, education and knowledge are "practices of care" that metastabilize transindividuation ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018/index.md>)).
- **Ars Industrialis *Vocabulaire*:** the pharmakon is both what enables care and what must be cared for ([glossary](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013/index.md>)).

## Disagreements

- **Lotz (2015):** the emphasis on "responsibility, care, spirit, and education" shows a position "idealistic and ethical" at its core, a reformist social-democratic ethics in place of political economy ([source](<https://lmmx.github.io/stiegler-reenchantment-of-the-world/sources/review-lotz-marx-and-philosophy-2015/index.md>)).
- **Cohen (2017)** is sympathetic but corrective. She uses Foucault's aesthetics of existence to temper Stiegler's more prescriptive mode.
- **Labelled interpretation:** the play on "economy" in French (*économiser*, to save or spare, to look after) carries much of the 2010 axiom's force. In English the link between economizing and caring is less evident.
