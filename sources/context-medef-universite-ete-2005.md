# The Medef 2005 summer university ("Réenchanter le monde")

## What could be established

- **The theme.** The Medef (the French employers' federation) held its 2005 *université d'été* under the banner "Réenchanter le monde". Three sources support this. (1) Ars Industrialis's own presentation of its 24 September 2005 session says the Medef summer university "a mis son programme de rencontres sous le signe de la nécessité de 'réenchanter le monde'" (node/1460). (2) Olivier Ezratty, a tech consultant who attended the 2005 edition as a spectator, lists the sequence of Medef summer-university titles: "Réenchanter le monde" (2005), "Concilier l'inconciliable" (2006), "Jouer le jeu" (2007), "Voir grand" (2008). (3) The French publisher's blurb, quoted on bookseller and reader sites, says that in 2005 the Medef held its summer university "sous la bannière du 'réenchantement du monde'".
- **Who chose and framed it, according to Stiegler.** Stiegler's 24 September 2005 talk (node/1933; see [primary-parallel-stiegler-colline-2005.md](primary-parallel-stiegler-colline-2005.md)) attributes the theme to **Ernest-Antoine Seillière**, Medef president until July 2005. He says **Denis Kessler**, a leading Medef figure, presented the theme by calling for an "âge d'une augmentation de la valeur esprit", that is, a greater role for knowledge in the economy. According to Stiegler, Kessler cited a **Tony Blair** speech proposing to redirect EU Common Agricultural Policy money towards knowledge technologies. Stiegler also cites Seillière's preface to the EIM volume *Les Dirigeants face au changement* (2004), the same book that contains Patrick Le Lay's "temps de cerveau humain disponible" remark (see [context-le-lay-temps-de-cerveau-2004.md](context-le-lay-temps-de-cerveau-2004.md)). Stiegler suggests the preface may have been written by advisers or by Kessler. The book's Introduction repeats this material (English edition pp. 2–3, per the existing primary-parallel file). A French excerpt of the Introduction quoted on the Charybde blog (see [review-hugues-charybde27-2024.md](review-hugues-charybde27-2024.md)) adds details. The theme served to promote the views of a Medef leadership "qui était alors en train de changer" on cognitive capitalism and knowledge industries. Kessler "en présentant cette université d'été" referred to Blair's proposal at a time when Britain held the EU presidency (second half of 2005). Seillière had himself wanted this summer university "juste avant de quitter la direction du Medef". The excerpt also sets out the book's question: is the Medef's re-enchantment a "sursaut de la valeur esprit", or a project for an industrial production of "connaissances sans esprit"?
- **Leadership.** Laurence Parisot succeeded Seillière as Medef president in July 2005, so the August 2005 summer university was held under her new presidency on a theme reportedly set by her predecessor. Her 2005–2013 tenure is confirmed by Wikipedia. Her exact role at the 2005 event was **not verified**. According to a third-party transcription recorded in [review-flammarion-4e-de-couverture-2006.md](review-flammarion-4e-de-couverture-2006.md), the French edition is dedicated "A la mémoire de Simon Nora" and "A Laurence Parisot". If that is correct, the book is pointedly addressed to the new Medef president.
- **Speakers.** Ezratty recalls being impressed by the speakers in 2005 and names only **Erik Orsenna** and **Canada's ambassador**, who spoke on state reform in Canada. I found no full programme. Searches for speeches by Prime Minister Villepin or Nicolas Sarkozy at the 2005 event gave no result, so **do not assert that either spoke**.
- **Venue and dates.** These were not verified. Later editions (2007–2008) were held at the HEC campus in Jouy-en-Josas or at École Polytechnique, but no source found here confirms the 2005 venue or exact dates (late August 2005 is likely but unconfirmed).

## What the Medef meant, as far as can be told

Every account available here comes through Stiegler or through readers repeating him. As he presents it, the Medef used "re-enchantment" as a positive slogan for the knowledge economy, the "société de la connaissance" of Blair and the Medef. Under that slogan, human capital and knowledge would replace the West's lost "technological rent" (Seillière's preface: "Ce début de siècle sonne la fin de la rente technologique pour l'Occident"). Stiegler and Ars Industrialis took the slogan "at its word" and turned it around. The same industrial milieu that wants re-enchantment also sells "available brain time", and that contradiction is the book's starting point. Stiegler set the slogan against Weber's "disenchantment" and Valéry's "baisse de la valeur esprit" (1939).

## Short quotations

- Ars Industrialis, presentation of the 24 Sept 2005 session: the Medef university placed its programme "sous le signe de la nécessité de 'réenchanter le monde'".
- Ezratty (2008): "'Voir grand' fait ainsi suite à 'Réenchanter le monde' (2005), 'Concilier l'inconciliable' (2006) et 'Jouer le jeu' (2007)."
- Kessler, as reported by Stiegler: "l'âge d'une augmentation de la valeur esprit".

## Notes for evaluation

- **Primary Medef documents were not found.** That means no programme, no Kessler text and no Blair reference checked at source. Kessler's and Seillière's statements are known here **only through Stiegler's citation**, so they should be cited as "according to Stiegler".
- Ezratty's blog is a reliable first-hand record of the sequence of titles, but it is a personal blog.
- Do not confuse this event with other 2005–2007 "réenchantement" uses: Jean Staune's science and spirituality writing, Michel Maffesoli's *Le Réenchantement du monde* (2007), and Joshua Landy and Michael Saler's *The Re-Enchantment of the World* (Stanford, 2009). These are unrelated to the Medef event or to Stiegler's book.
