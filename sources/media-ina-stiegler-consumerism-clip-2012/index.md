# INA clip, "Bernard Stiegler rises against consumerism" (2012)

A 1 min 38 s clip from France Télévisions' *Mots de minuit*, catalogued by INA with a short description.

## Condensed account

Philippe Lefait interviews Stiegler about *États de choc* (2012). The description calls Stiegler co-founder of Ars Industrialis and says that, as early as 2005, the association predicted "the collapse of the consumerism and contemporary capitalism system".

## Notes for evaluation

It is relevant only as an example of how French media framed Ars Industrialis after 2008: as prophets of the end of consumerism, dated to 2005. It says nothing about *Réenchanter le monde*. The "predicted in 2005" framing is a media gloss, though it agrees with the inaugural session's diagnosis of a libidinal crisis of capitalism.
