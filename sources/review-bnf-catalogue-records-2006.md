## Notes for evaluation
- **FR/EN structural differences that can be established:** (1) The 2006 French edition cannot contain the **2010 Manifesto**, which is an EN addition. It is unclear whether the 2008 Champs reissue added anything. (2) There is no translator's preface in FR. (3) The Eyrolles snippet suggests that "Le sursaut" may be a section heading with subsections nested under it in FR, whereas the EN contents list it as a flat section. (4) Possible translation point: the snippet's "Le **plan** qui m'enchante" against EN "the **plane** that enchants me". This needs verification, because the snippet was not seen in context and may itself be garbled.
- Whether the French edition includes the **Tunis motion** was not confirmed by any French source seen. EN publisher copy mentions "an account of the organisation's 2005 summit in Tunis".
- Treat everything in the table marked "snippet" as provisional.
