## Notes for evaluation
- **FR/EN structural differences:** (1) The **2010 Manifesto** is an English addition; the 2006 French edition predates it. (2) There is no translator's preface in French. (3) The Eyrolles listing nests "Grammatisation et individuation" under "Le sursaut : entreprendre autrement", whereas the English contents list both as flat sections. (4) "Le **plan** qui m'enchante" becomes "the **plane** that enchants me": a correct rendering, since the section is about a plane of consistencies.
- The French edition prints the **Tunis motion** at FR p. 163 (cited in Ch. 2 n. 6). English publisher copy describes it as "an account of the organisation's 2005 summit in Tunis".
