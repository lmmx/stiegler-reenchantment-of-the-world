# Heulot, "Réenchanter le monde, le programme de Bernard Stiegler" (Rennes, 2014)

A four-session popular philosophy workshop in Rennes. The society published a presentation and a bibliography for it.

## Condensed account (presentation text)

- The workshop starts from the present crisis, which it dates back to **Ford in the 1910s**. It presents Stiegler as thinking the causes of the **disenchantment of the world**, in which a financial and economic rationality wholly given over to marketing leads to global catastrophe.
- Its themes: reason turned against itself; a capitalism that extends its power while destroying itself; **desire replaced by drive**, leaving people "disaffected and dismissed from their existences".
- Re-enchantment requires thinking industrial technology as a **pharmakon**, both poison and remedy, so as to "**re-found society**" and fight "**against the reign of ignorance**". These are the French titles of Ch. 1 and Ch. 2. The "valeur esprit" must become an object of technical, financial and political **investment**.
- Guiding questions: what is this disenchanted world, what is the diagnosis, what are the causes, what remedies, what can still be hoped for?
- The bibliography gives the book as Flammarion Champs-Essais, alongside *De la misère symbolique*, *La télécratie contre la démocratie*, *Ce qui fait que la vie vaut la peine d'être vécue*, *Pharmacologie du Front national*, *Mécréance et discrédit* 1–3, *Pour une nouvelle critique de l'économie politique*, and Horkheimer and Adorno's *Dialectic of Enlightenment*.

## Notes for evaluation

- **Value:** evidence of French *practitioner* reception. A community philosophy workshop of four sessions took the book as its organizing text in 2014. It independently confirms the French part titles "refonder la société" and "contre le règne de l'ignorance".
- **Stance:** sympathetic and pedagogical. Like Lyons, it frames the book through the later *pharmakon* vocabulary.
- **Reliability:** accurate as far as it goes. A short announcement, depth 1.
