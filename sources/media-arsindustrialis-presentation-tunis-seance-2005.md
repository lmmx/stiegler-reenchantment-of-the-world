# Announcement and argument for the 24 September 2005 session

**Nature of source.** This is the announcement and argument page for the session, read in full. It links to Stiegler's talk text (node/1933, analysed in [primary-parallel-stiegler-colline-2005.md](primary-parallel-stiegler-colline-2005.md)) and to a Japanese translation of it.

## Condensed account

- **Purpose.** The session will not just inform members about the UN summit (16–18 Nov 2005). Ars Industrialis, acting as a legal person, will draft a **public motion** to intervene in the European political, economic and industrial debate. The summit is treated as an occasion to raise the "politique industrielle des technologies de l'esprit".
- **Terms.** The page notes the vocabulary: "société de savoir" (UNESCO) and "société de la connaissance" (attributed to "Anthony Blair" and the MEDEF). It equates this with "cognitive capitalism", where all questions about **hypomnémata** now converge. Tunis should therefore address an industrial politics of hypomnémata, that is, a politics of spirit.
- **The paradox.** People speak of knowledge societies at the very moment when the "avilissement des esprits (devenus du temps de cerveau disponible)" is openly claimed by part of industry in the name of economic "realism". Meanwhile the Medef summer university was placed under the sign of "réenchanter le monde". The page links this to Weber's disenchantment and the "spirit of capitalism", and then to Valéry's 1939 "baisse de la valeur esprit".
- **First hypothesis.** The issue needs ambitious theory, made concrete in a long-term European policy carried out by "une nouvelle forme de puissance publique".
- **Format.** The session is moderated by Catherine Perret, with Jean-Max Noyer, Serge Regourd and Stiegler. A further session on 5 November will cover search engines, databases and digitised collections. Proposed working groups: suffering and consumption; property and the economy of free software; the future of television; information society and industrial policy; search engines.
- **Method.** Industrial competition imposes "unsustainable" rates of socialisation of ICTs, which produce informational inflation and the "cognitive overflow syndrom" (saturation). The association wants to use digital tools without a "pratique maniaque".

## Short quotations

- "l'université d'été du MEDEF a mis son programme de rencontres sous le signe de la nécessité de 'réenchanter le monde'".
- "l'avilissement des esprits (devenus du temps de cerveau disponible)".

## Notes for evaluation

- This is the earliest dated document found that **joins the Medef slogan, Le Lay's "available brain time", Weber and Valéry** in a single argument. It amounts to the seed of the book's Introduction.
- Its "cognitive overflow" passage anticipates ch. 2's "cognitive saturation" section.
- It is unsigned, but it is clearly the official voice of the association.
