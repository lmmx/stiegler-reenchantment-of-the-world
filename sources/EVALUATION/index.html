Evaluation of sources¶

Home › Source evaluation · see also reception.md

Each source in sources/ is rated on five criteria:

  • Accuracy: against the book itself (see STRUCTURE.md).
  • Coverage: which parts of the book it treats: P = Preface, I = Introduction, M05 / M10 = Manifestos, C1 / C2 = Chapters, T = Tunis motion.
  • Depth: how far the source goes, from 1 (notice) to 5 (sustained analysis).
  • Stance: interpretive bias.
  • Reliability: high / medium / low.

The files themselves hold the condensed account and fuller notes.

A. Primary texts and primary-parallel texts (authorial)¶

E. Cross-source disagreements¶

Issue Positions Our assessment
Is the remedy empty? Lotz: "empty placeholders". Lyons: "philosophy of hope" but no concrete answers. Lemmens interview: a "new industrial model" with policy planks Both reviewers under-weight the 2010 Manifesto §9 policies and Ch. 2's "practical consequences". Those are concrete but sectoral (education, media, R&D instruments) rather than an account of political agency
Marx Lotz, Hutnyk, Manche: class and accumulation sidelined. Ross, Lemmens: return to the Grundrisse and the knowledge sense of proletarianization A real disagreement of frameworks; see concepts/proletarianization.md
Spirit Roodt: reduced to libido. De Beer: spiritual depth. Abbinnett v. Beardsworth: memorial v. futural. Rossouw: theological The book's own definition is noetic and transindividual and explicitly not only religious (Tunis motion)
Digital networks 2010 Manifesto: associated milieu. Stiegler 2018: "systemically dissociating" The author revised his view; read the 2010 text historically
Authorship / founders EN Wikipedia: three co-authors. Catalogues and association: five founders Five (Collins, Crépon, Perret, B. Stiegler, C. Stiegler)
Tunis motion Lyons, blurbs: "at the summit", "account of the summit". Association: press motion on the eve The association's account is correct

F. Ranked reading list¶

Ordered by value for someone reading the book now. Primary texts first, because their accuracy is not in question.

  1. primary-parallel-stiegler-colline-2005: the draft of Ch. 2. Read it before or alongside the chapter.
  2. primary-parallel-stiegler-uncontrollable-societies-nja-2013: the best contemporaneous statement of spirit, Medef and industrial populism.
  3. review-lotz-marx-and-philosophy-2015: the best page-located review, and the sharpest critique of the book itself.
  4. lit-ross-neganthropocene-introduction-2018: places the book in the corpus; the author's own later revision.
  5. glossary-arsindustrialis-vocabulaire-2013: house definitions. Mind the dating.
  6. lit-manche-proletarianisation-not-capitalism-2021: the strongest critique of proletarianization and contribution.
  7. lit-cohen-dare-to-care-2017: Valéry's esprit and care.
  8. review-roodt-tydskrif-vir-geesteswetenskappe-2012: the Arendtian challenge to "life of the mind".
  9. lit-lemmens-digital-pharmakon-general-intellect-2017: a sympathetic critique of statism.
  10. primary-parallel-stiegler-pharmacology-of-desire-2011: the libidinal-economy thesis at full strength.
  11. lit-abbinnett-politics-of-spirit-2015: the Derridean reading of spirit.
  12. review-debeer-tydskrif-vir-geesteswetenskappe-2012: French page anchors.
  13. review-lyons-critical-inquiry-2014: the sympathetic Anglophone reception.
  14. context-le-lay-temps-de-cerveau-2004 and context-medef-universite-ete-2005: needed to follow the Introduction.
  15. lit-hutnyk-proletarianisation-2012, lit-hansen-saving-libidinal-capitalism-2014, lit-hui-halpin-collective-individuation-2013: further angles.
  16. Everything else is reception colour or bibliographic support.
