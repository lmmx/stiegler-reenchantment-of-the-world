# Swiss teaching sheet on *Le Temps de cerveau disponible* (2010)

## Condensed account

A teaching guide for upper-secondary and teacher-training use. It covers the 57-minute documentary directed by Jean-Robert Viallet and produced by Christophe Nick, broadcast on France 3 on 17 March 2010 and on TSR2 on 26 Sept 2010. The film traces TV entertainment from the 1980s to reality TV and argues that programmes exploit drives and transgression to sell "available brain time". It calls the film an effective indictment, "notamment grâce aux réflexions du philosophe Bernard Stiegler". It also faults the film's shortcut conclusion about "social war". A box presents Stiegler as founder of the IRI at the Centre Pompidou and convenor of Ars Industrialis, and links to his pages on arsindustrialis.org. The lesson plan asks teachers to put Le Lay's sentence back in its context, an interview for the book *Les dirigeants face au changement*, using Acrimed's full reproduction (article1688). It also asks them to return to "le discours de Stiegler sur les pulsions, de vie et de mort", and asks what a "promesse sadique" is. The further reading includes Stiegler's *La Télécratie contre la démocratie* (2008).

## Short quotations

- The film is an effective indictment "notamment grâce aux réflexions du philosophe Bernard Stiegler".

## Notes for evaluation

- This is evidence of the **pedagogical afterlife** of the Le Lay and Stiegler pairing. The "available brain time" critique reached schools via the documentary, not via *Réenchanter le monde* itself, which the sheet does not cite.
- It is accurate on the Le Lay source (interview in *Les dirigeants face au changement*). It relies on Acrimed.
- I read it via text extraction of the PDF. The layout is multi-column, so a few sentence joins may be imperfect.
