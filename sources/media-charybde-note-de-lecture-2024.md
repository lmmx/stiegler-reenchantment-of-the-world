# Charybde blog, reading note (2024)

## Condensed account

A short, enthusiastic note by a Paris bookseller (Librairie Charybde). The blogger's own commentary has three parts:

- A one-line verdict.
- A biographical sketch of Stiegler: born 1952, died 2020; imprisoned in Toulouse 1978–1983 for an armed robbery undertaken, the blogger says, to save his rock bar from bankruptcy; came to philosophy late, at 26, with Derrida's support; founded Ars Industrialis in 2005 and the IRI at Beaubourg in 2006.
- A framing of the book. Published in 2006 by Flammarion under Stiegler's name together with the collective Ars Industrialis, it is a manifesto and a first milestone of a long project. It was spurred by the Medef's 2005 summer university slogan "Réenchanter le monde", and it is an "indispensable right of reply" that goes far beyond that occasion.

Most of the post, roughly three-quarters, is **long excerpts from the book**: Valéry and the decline of the "valeur esprit"; attention and TV and connected devices; the Medef, Kessler, Blair, Seillière's preface, and Le Lay; the book's framing question; and a passage from the 2010 manifesto. The blogger praises the book's reading of "what has been at work in our imaginaries as workers and consumers" for some thirty years.

## Short quotations (blogger's own words)

- "Un formidable manifeste philosophique et politique de 2006 pour mieux appréhender et combattre l'emprise de l'imaginaire capitaliste".
- The book "propose une lecture rare et tonique" of contemporary imaginaries.

## Notes for evaluation

- **Accurate on the context.** The 2005 Medef slogan, the 2006 Flammarion edition and the co-signature with Ars Industrialis are all correct. Its long excerpts are a convenient way to see the French wording of the Introduction (Medef/Kessler/Blair/Seillière/Le Lay), but they are not a substitute for the book. The excerpts are the publisher's text, reproduced at length.
- The biographical colour (the bar, the robbery) is standard and broadly reported elsewhere. It is not relevant to the book.
- Minor slip: the title is typed once as "Rééchanter le monde".
- **Stance:** wholly favourable and non-critical. It is reader reception, not analysis.
