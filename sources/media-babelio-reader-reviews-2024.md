# Babelio reader reception

## Condensed account

- **Stats when read:** average 3.5/5 from 24 ratings; about 194 readers list the book; 3 written reviews. The page's counts do not fully add up.
- **Editions listed:** Flammarion (Oct 2006, c. 172 pp.) and Flammarion "Champs" paperback (Oct 2008, c. 176 pp.).
- **Blurb (truncated on page):** it opens with Valéry's 1939 "baisse de la valeur esprit" and states that only 45% of French people then listened to the radio and that TV did not yet exist. It contrasts this with today's connected devices competing for attention.
- **Reviews:** (1) **Charybde2** (Feb 2024): the same text as the Charybde blog note (see [media-charybde-note-de-lecture-2024.md](media-charybde-note-de-lecture-2024.md)). (2) **Khalil_Livres** (June 2024): summarises the book as a critique of the declining "valeur esprit" tied to passive TV consumption, and as a plea for individuals to regain the capacity to shape their lives with the support of public policy. (3) **Kadjagoogoo** (May 2017): a YouTube link only, no text.

## Short quotations

- Khalil_Livres (quoting or paraphrasing the book's theme): "Une menace contre le désir est une menace contre l'humanité tout entière."

## Notes for evaluation

There is little reception here. The French readers who engage are sympathetic and summarise rather than criticise. The useful datum is the edition history (2006 first edition; 2008 "Champs" paperback). That history explains why some sites, for example Unidivers in a search snippet, give **2008** as the publication date. That date is the paperback, not the first edition.
